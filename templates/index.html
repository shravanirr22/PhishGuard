Security Tips

Best practices to protect yourself from phishing attacks

🔍

Verify the URL

Always check the URL carefully. Look for misspellings of trusted domains (e.g., g00gle.com vs google.com).

🔒

Check for HTTPS

While HTTPS alone doesn't guarantee safety, HTTP sites should raise immediate suspicion for sensitive actions.

📧

Beware of Email Links

Never click links in unsolicited emails. Instead, type the URL directly into your browser.

🚫

Don't Enter Credentials

Never enter passwords or personal information on a site you arrived at via a suspicious link.

🛡️

Use 2FA

Enable two-factor authentication on all important accounts to mitigate the impact of credential theft.

📱

Report Phishing

Report suspicious URLs to your browser vendor, Google Safe Browsing, or Microsoft SmartScreen.
